My name is Elisabetta and I work in IT for an insurance group, across security, infrastructure and code.

## From developer to security and infrastructure

I started as a full stack developer and I still write software: with Insureteach I have worked on Insurelab, the online courses and academy platform, and on the YourSpace CRM, from functional requirements to release. Over time I moved towards security and infrastructure, and today I see technology from both sides: how it is built, and how it is run, secured and governed.

At Edge Group I work hands-on on the two Microsoft 365 tenants of a group of around twenty companies and 350 users. I handle VAPT remediation, cross-tenant collaboration compliant with IVASS segregation, migrations to SharePoint and the company smartphone fleet. Alongside the technical work I coordinate vendors and colleagues on larger projects, such as the IT integration of acquired companies, budget and procedures.

## The full picture

I like to understand a system as a whole: how it is configured, what it costs, who relies on it every day and how to explain it to management. That is also what I am trying to do in my MSc thesis in Computer Engineering at the University of Trieste. It is called O.Di.Ri.S.: an LLM-based voice agent guides incident reporting in healthcare facilities, and the data feeds an insurance-related clinical risk index.

## Outside work

I am curious by nature. In the kitchen I experiment with recipes from all over the world, I play the piano (I studied at the Conservatorio of Vittorio Veneto) and in my free time I browse flea markets for objects with a story. That is where my passion for design began, and also the generative compositions at the top of this site: every visit draws a new one.

## What you will find here

I will write about Microsoft 365 security and infrastructure, migrations and IT integrations, development and what I learn along the way. If you want to talk about any of it, email me: it is the fastest way to reach me.
