I'm Elisabetta, and I look after Microsoft 365 security and infrastructure for an insurance group. This is the first post on the site, so it seems right to start by introducing myself.

## Where I come from

I started out writing software and I never stopped. At Insureteach I develop Insurelab, the platform that hosts its courses and academies, and YourSpace, the CRM and client portal.

Over time I moved closer to what sits underneath the applications: identities, networks, devices, permissions. Today I know both sides of technology, how a system is built and how to keep it secure and under control, and I think that is what makes me useful.

## What I do today

At Edge Group I manage the two Microsoft 365 tenants of a group of some twenty companies and around 350 people. I am coordinating the remediation of this year's VAPT, I set up collaboration between the two tenants while keeping the segregation the Italian insurance regulator (IVASS) requires, and I rolled out 200 smartphones managed with Knox and Intune.

A large part of the job is not technical. I bring acquired companies into the group, coordinate vendors and colleagues, and look after budgets and procedures. With every system, I want to know how it works, what it costs and who depends on it, so I can explain it to the people who make the decisions.

## My thesis

I am completing my MSc in Computer Engineering at the University of Trieste. My thesis is O.Di.Ri.S., a platform built with Insureteach to measure insurance-related clinical risk in healthcare facilities: an LLM-based voice agent guides incident reporting, and the data it collects feeds a risk index.

## Outside work

I play the piano, which I studied at the Conservatorio of Vittorio Veneto. I cook recipes from all over the world, and in my free time I browse flea markets for objects with a story. That is where my love of design comes from, and the generative compositions at the top of this site too: every visit draws a new one.

## What I'll write about

Microsoft 365 security and infrastructure, migrations and IT integrations, development, and whatever I learn along the way. If you'd like to talk about any of it, send me an email.
